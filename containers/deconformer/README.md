## Paper
- **Title:** Pathway-enhanced Transformer-based robust model for quantifying cell types of origin of cell-free transcriptome
- **Journal:** bioRxiv (preprint), 2024
- **DOI:** [10.1101/2024.02.28.582494](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.02.28.582494)
- **Authors:** Shuo Yan, Xuetao Tian, Yulong Qin, Jiayi Li, Tingyu Yang, Dongyue Yu, Wentao Liu, Jinghua Sun, Chengbin Hu, Qing Zhou, Zhongzhen Liu, Wen-Jing Wang
- **Affiliation:** BGI Research, Shenzhen & University of Chinese Academy of Sciences

## Description
Deconformer is a Transformer-based model for deconvolution of cell-free RNA (cfRNA) transcriptome data. It integrates biological signaling pathways directly into the embedding stage via a pathway-based mask matrix. The model has three components: (1) pathway-based embedding, (2) Transformer encoder, (3) cell-type fraction prediction.
